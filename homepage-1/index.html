    LISTENING TO THE RHYTHMS OF MACHINES THINKING

    Song Dong is an Interdisciplinary Designer working at the intersection of AI’s perceptual infrastructures, computational design, and human–computer interaction. As an architect, media artist, and HCI researcher, they build embodied systems that surface the hidden temporal and material logics of machine reasoning—reframing how humans understand, negotiate, and intervene in algorithmic processes.
