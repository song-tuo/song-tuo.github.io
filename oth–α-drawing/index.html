    OTH–α: Drawing

    Instructor: Ewa Harabasz
    Personal Project
    Tools & Technologies: pencils, vine charcoal, markers, ink, and other wet and dry media




    Introduction
    The objective of A Window is to investigate the interaction between the static, restrictive geometry of an architectural window and the dynamic, expressive structure of the human body. Through extreme bodily movement, the project explores how the body can temporarily inhabit, imitate, and challenge architectural form, translating moments of force and motion into a full-scale drawing that captures this embodied architectural exchange.


    Introduction
    The objective of the sketch is to capture the moment of transformation, focusing on the peak of energy when an object changes form. Rather than emphasizing the before and after states, the work centers on the act of change itself, using photography to reveal forces and transitions that are usually invisible.  (Model: Madeleine Farrer)
